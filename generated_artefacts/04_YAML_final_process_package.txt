      name: Tactical Point-of-Injury Trauma Stabilization
      parent_domain: D2
      related_domains: [D4, D6]
      related_findings: [E1, E13]
      prioritization_result:
        overall_priority: High
        key_selection_factors:
          - Direct dependency on real-time battlefield physiological status telemetry streams.
          - Highly specified, sequential frontline treatment milestones in doctrine.
      selection_rationale: Immediate point-of-injury treatment is the core operational layer where real-time physiological alerts directly change clinical outcomes and mitigate preventable mortality.

      process_charter:
        purpose: Stabilize life-threatening battlefield injuries at the point of tactical incident using first responder care protocols.
        process_boundary:
          start_condition: A responder or soldier sustains an acute physical injury or health threat in the operational field.
          end_state: The patient is clinically stabilized, documented, and prepared for evacuation handover.
        included_scope:
          - Immediate care under fire threat suppression and movement to cover.
          - Application of primary life-saving interventions including hemorrhage and airway control.
          - Tactical field care documentation using standardized casualty cards.
        excluded_scope:
          - Advanced surgical operations or facility-based clinical admission procedures.
          - Coordination of long-range transport fleet assets or flight path routing.
        main_actors:
          - Combat Medic or First Responder
          - Buddy Aid Provider
          - Casualty (Patient)
        potential_bpmn_lanes:
          - First Responder / Combat Medic
          - Patient / Casualty
        core_process_logic:
          - Perform care under fire steps to neutralize local threat vectors.
          - Apply tourniquets and primary airway stabilization under cover.
          - Evaluate mental status and verify neurological responsiveness levels.
          - Complete standard tactical combat casualty care card records for transport.
        key_decisions_or_handovers:
          - Decision whether to treat in place under active threat versus moving to localized cover.
          - Direct clinical handover of completed casualty metrics to incoming extraction teams.
        expected_outcome: Casualty is structurally stabilized against immediate threats to life and prepped for tactical evacuation.

        potential_bpmn_lanes:
          - Field Medical Unit
          - Evacuation Coordinating Cell
          - Dispatch Dispatcher
        core_process_logic:
          - Collate grid location, radio frequency, call sign, and patient counts.

          - Transmit structured 9-Line payload over secure combat radio channels.
        key_decisions_or_handovers:
          - Decision to assign Urgent versus Priority precedence based on vital status.
          - Communication handover between tactical unit and regional dispatcher.
        expected_outcome: Accurate MEDEVAC request is received and logged, leading to immediate evacuation vehicle dispatch.
