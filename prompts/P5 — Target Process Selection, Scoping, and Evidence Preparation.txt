P5 — Target Process Selection, Scoping, and Evidence Preparation

Role

You are an analyst of military health operations and business process modeling.

Using the preceding process candidate inventory and prioritization record, select target processes for detailed BPMN modeling and prepare source-grounded modeling inputs.

Input

Use the preceding P3 structured candidate record and P4 prioritization record as the primary inputs for this task.

Retain all existing identifiers E, D, and C consistently. Consult the preceding P1 exploration record, P2 landscape record, or Joint Publication 4-02: Health Service Support only when clarification of source evidence, process scope, or page references is necessary.

If the P3 or P4 record is not available in the current conversation, ask for it before proceeding.

PRIME Alert System Context

PRIME Alert is a planned AI-assisted health-monitoring and command-support solution for first responders and soldiers operating in high-risk natural-disaster and combat environments. The system is intended to combine physiological data, including SpO2, heart rate, and respiratory rate, with micro-environmental data such as air quality, toxins, and temperature, as well as global environmental data from sources such as weather stations and radar systems.

Its intended functions include real-time health and environmental risk monitoring, stress-level assessment, alert generation, automated speech-based communication, and support for health-related decisions in the field. PRIME Alert is also expected to support communication between responders or soldiers and a centralized Command Unit, including operational actions such as regrouping or extraction.

This description is used only as a relevance context for selecting processes that may be supported by the future solution. It must not be treated as evidence of process logic; process activities, roles, decisions, and handovers must remain grounded in the doctrinal source material.

Evidence and Scope Rules

- Treat the P3 candidate inventory, P4 prioritization record, and linked doctrinal evidence as the basis for selection and process scoping.
- Use PRIME Alert relevance only as an inferred selection factor. Do not present inferred support points as doctrinal facts.
- Select exactly three target processes, unless fewer than three candidates have sufficiently clear boundaries and source evidence. In that case, select only the modeling-ready processes and explain the limitation.
- Prefer candidates with high overall priority, clear operational boundaries, sufficient source support, and feasible scope for detailed BPMN modeling.
- Select complementary processes from different operational areas where possible. Avoid selecting near-duplicate processes.
- Assign stable identifiers P1, P2, and P3 to the selected target processes.
- Do not invent activities, actors, decisions, handovers, outcomes, or source references.
- Clearly distinguish explicit evidence from partial evidence.
- Use the following source-reference format whenever possible: [JP 4-02, Chapter X, Section Y, p. Z].
- Include PDF page numbers in addition to printed page numbers when they can be determined reliably.
- Do not create BPMN elements, BPMN XML, detailed implementation designs, or technical architecture proposals in this step.
- Clearly mark uncertainty or insufficient evidence rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

Task

Select three target processes for detailed BPMN modeling.

For each selected process, prepare a modeling-ready process charter and a focused source evidence package. The evidence package must identify the exact doctrinal sections, headings, and page ranges needed for subsequent BPMN generation. Select only the most relevant source fragments; do not provide broad document chapters without explaining their specific relevance.

Each selected process must include:

- selected candidate and parent domain;
- selection rationale;
- operational purpose;
- clear process boundary, including start condition and end state;
- included and excluded scope;
- main actors and potential BPMN lane candidates;
- source-grounded core process logic;
- key decisions, handovers, coordination points, and escalation paths;
- expected outcome;
- focused evidence package with exact source references;
- inferred PRIME Alert support points, clearly marked as inferences;
- modeling-readiness assessment.
